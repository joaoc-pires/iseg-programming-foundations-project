📦 The data

Monthly "Flights" files from EUROCONTROL, November 2024 to October 2025. process_lisbon_flights.py keeps the flights departing Lisbon (ICAO code LPPT), and analyse_lisbon_flights.py calculates the statistics shown in this app.

Only scheduled flights (ICAO flight type "S") are analysed.

📏 What "delay" means here

Deviation = actual time minus the filed flight-plan time. Positive means later than filed. Filed times are not always the times shown to passengers, so these numbers are flight-plan deviations, not passenger delays.

A flight is on time when it is at most 15 minutes late, the industry standard. This includes early flights.

🕐 Times

All times are UTC, as given by EUROCONTROL. Lisbon is UTC+0 in winter and UTC+1 in summer, so in summer a 09:00 local departure appears as 08:00.

🔎 Reading the tables

Airlines and destinations are ICAO codes (e.g. TAP, LEMD). The code ZZZ means the operator was not disclosed; it is not an airline, so it is left out of the airline ranking.

Groups with fewer than 100 flights are left out, because their percentages are unreliable. Extreme values are kept; the median is shown next to the mean because a few very large values do not move it.
